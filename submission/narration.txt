Industrial reliability has a bottleneck. When a machine misbehaves, the alarm tells an engineer what changed, not what to review next, and the knowledge is scattered across datasheets, register maps and event logs. One upstream fault becomes many tickets. Kairos AI is an agentic reliability platform that turns a device's own documents and telemetry into cited, actionable diagnoses. It is built on Google Cloud and deployed on Cloud Run. This is the real application. All telemetry and documents are synthetic, and nothing controls real equipment.

An administrator onboards a device and feeds it knowledge: datasheets, spreadsheets, notes, or links to GitHub and Confluence. Every document is extracted, chunked and embedded with Vertex AI, then stored as a private, per-device corpus in Firestore. This is retrieval augmented generation, so answers stay grounded in that device's own documentation.

Ask the device is an agentic assistant with three modes: ask, plan and agent. It clarifies a vague request before acting, then it can generate physically consistent test datasets, test cases, code, or a live monitoring plan. Gemini reasons over the retrieved evidence and cites its sources instead of inventing them.

Analysis runs a five agent engine: monitor, diagnose, decide, act and verify. On a dataset with ground truth scenarios it reports diagnosis accuracy, estimated downtime avoided and cost avoided. Offline fallbacks are always labeled and never presented as Google AI.

Live monitoring closes the loop. The agent reads the device's datasheet to decide which fields to poll, decodes event log codes, and streams readings into the very same engine used for historic analysis. One engine, two modes, live and historic.

Under the hood, Cloud Run hosts the app with a dedicated service account and no embedded keys. Gemini on Vertex AI does the reasoning. Vertex embeddings and Firestore hold the knowledge and platform state. Cloud Storage and BigQuery hold telemetry and a partitioned audit trail.

We are honest about limits. The demo data is synthetic, live modeling treats each device as a single asset, and we claim no production savings. One hundred and twenty one automated tests cover the workflow. Our next step is an approved pilot that measures triage time, engineer acceptance and escalation quality. Kairos AI gives manufacturing teams evidence to action, with humans in control.
