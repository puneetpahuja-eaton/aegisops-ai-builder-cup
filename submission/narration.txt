A plant alarm tells you what changed, not what to review next. Kairos AI turns operational telemetry into a cited incident hypothesis and an engineer handoff. This is an edited screen sequence from the real deployed application. Network waiting time is omitted. All telemetry and procedures are synthetic. Nothing controls real equipment.

The application runs on Google Cloud Run. Gemini on Vertex AI reasons over the incident evidence and procedure passages. Private Cloud Storage holds the documents and scenarios. BigQuery preserves the audit records. The deployed application uses a dedicated service account rather than an embedded API key.

Here is the motor incident. Its winding temperature and vibration deviate from their baselines. The monitor computes robust Z scores, which detect deviation rather than predict a failure probability. Gemini identifies a possible thermal management issue and a concurrent vibration condition. It selects numbered source passages. The application validates those references and copies the actual text from the procedure, instead of letting the model invent quotations. Root causes remain hypotheses that an engineer must review.

The independent decision gate checks asset criticality, physical repair codes, severe vibration, confidence and citation presence. Eligible cases still require a named supervisor to approve the sandbox scenario. After approval, the verifier evaluates the supplied post action readings. This is synthetic recovery, not a production repair. If readings are missing, the case stays unverified. If anomalies remain, it reopens.

The pump incident has a bearing risk code. The deterministic gate therefore requires engineer review, regardless of the model's recommendation. Gemini drafts a structured, review only work order using the validated evidence and procedure. Nothing is dispatched automatically, and the system does not issue physical servicing instructions.

The audit history records the model source, decision, human approval and verification. Operators can download the incident report and work orders. Model or storage failures are visible, and unavailable Google AI reasoning is never silently replaced by offline rules.

Twenty two automated tests cover the workflow and dashboard. The live cloud smoke test accepted four diagnoses and persisted four audit records. These are small synthetic checks, not proof of production accuracy or savings. Our next step is an approved pilot measuring triage time, engineer acceptance and handoff completeness. Kairos AI gives manufacturing teams evidence to action, with humans in control.
